# FarmIQ AI - Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## Product Name

FarmIQ AI – Farm Intelligence & Smart Selling Assistant

---

## Problem Statement

Farmers often make crop management and selling decisions without access to timely weather insights, crop intelligence, and market information.

This leads to:

* Reduced crop yield
* Higher losses
* Lower profitability
* Poor selling decisions

---

## Vision

Help farmers maximize profitability through AI-powered farm intelligence and smart selling recommendations.

---

## Target Users

### Primary Users

Small and medium farmers (1–20 acres)

### Secondary Users

* Agriculture consultants
* Agri-businesses
* FPOs (Farmer Producer Organizations)

---

## Goals

1. Improve farming decisions
2. Reduce crop losses
3. Increase farmer profitability
4. Provide market intelligence

---

## Non-Goals

* Farm loan management
* Insurance services
* Equipment marketplace

---

## MVP Features

### Feature 1: Farm Registration

Users can register:

* Crop type
* Farm size
* Location
* Sowing date

---

### Feature 2: Weather Intelligence

Display:

* Temperature
* Humidity
* Rainfall forecast

---

### Feature 3: AI Farm Advisor

Users can ask:

"Should I irrigate this week?"

The AI provides recommendations.

---

### Feature 4: Smart Selling Assistant

Provides:

* Current crop price
* Price trend
* Selling recommendation

---

## Success Metrics

### Activation

Farm registered successfully

### Engagement

Weekly active users

### Retention

Returning users every week

### Business Metric

Estimated increase in farmer profit

---

## North Star Metric

Additional farmer profit generated per season
